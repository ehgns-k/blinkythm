New music, better blink detection and a fixed calibration screen.

- Every game, menu, rest and result screen now has its own music, written by the project author. Each game's tempo follows its song: Lighthouse 92, Owl Choir 87, Blink Studio 102 BPM. The result screen plays a jingle for your stamp, then that rank's music.
- Blinks are read from MediaPipe Face Landmarker's blink scores instead of eye landmarks, which smoothed fast blinks away. A single frame below the threshold now counts, and every camera frame is processed during games and setup.
- New blink sensitivity setting (Low, Normal, High) in Settings and on the camera screen.
- The camera screen shows a live graph of eye openness with the threshold line, a mark for each detected blink and a counter, so missed blinks are easy to spot.
- Calibration: the metronome no longer goes silent after about two minutes. The arrow on the ring now follows the offset that would be applied, live, and a dim notch marks the saved offset.

Unzip `Blinkythm-v1.3.0-win64.zip` and run `Blinkythm.exe`. Requires Windows 10 or later (64-bit), a webcam and speakers or headphones. The exe is not signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn; choose "More info" and "Run anyway".

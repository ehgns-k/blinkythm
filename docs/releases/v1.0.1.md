Three minigames replace the single copy-the-rhythm round.

- Lighthouse (copy), Owl Choir (continue) and Blink Studio (2 beats later), about 35 seconds each, plus a medley of all three.
- Each game has its own backing track, cue sounds and pixel art scene. All art and sound are generated by `tools/make_assets.py`.
- The result screen shows accuracy per section and a one-line comment. Best scores are saved per game.
- The prompt opens the game after the one you played last.

Unzip `Blinkythm-v1.0.1-win64.zip` and run `Blinkythm.exe`. Requires Windows 10 or later (64-bit), a webcam and speakers or headphones. The exe is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn; choose "More info" and "Run anyway".
